# Spendora - Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

Spendora is a simple SaaS application that helps individuals and small teams record, organize, and monitor their expenses in one place.

## 2. Goal

Make expense tracking fast, clear, and accessible from any device.

## 3. Target Users

- Individuals tracking personal expenses
- Freelancers and small business owners
- Small teams managing shared expenses

## 4. MVP Features

- User registration, login, and logout
- Create, edit, and delete expenses
- Assign a category, date, amount, and note to each expense
- View expenses in a simple dashboard
- Filter expenses by date and category
- Display total spending and a basic category breakdown
- Support Arabic and English interfaces

## 5. Main Screens

- Landing page
- Sign up and login
- Dashboard
- Add or edit expense
- Expense history
- Account settings

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

- Responsive on mobile and desktop
- Secure user authentication and data separation
- Simple, fast, and accessible interface
- Store monetary values accurately

## 7. Out of Scope for the MVP

- Bank account integrations
- Automated receipt scanning
- Advanced accounting features
- Native mobile applications
- Multiple paid subscription plans

## 8. Success Metrics

- A new user can add their first expense in under two minutes
- Users can easily understand their total spending
- Expense creation and dashboard loading are reliable and fast

## 9. Future Ideas

- Budgets and spending alerts
- Recurring expenses
- Receipt uploads and OCR
- Reports and data export
- Team roles and approval workflows
- Subscription plans and billing
